# MIZAN Website Design Analysis

## Source Content Hierarchy

1. MIZAN is the primary product and brand.
   - Core line: "The record of who authorised the machine."
   - Category: institutional authority assurance for public-sector AI agents.
   - MIZAN maintains a reconstructable assurance case around authority, evidence, accountability, conditions and expiry.

2. The AI Agents for Public Purpose Lab / Manfa'a is supporting institutional context.
   - The Lab is an applied-research and delivery venture hosted by Arrownex.
   - It contains MIZAN, Qudra, Mu'tamad, Isnad and Misbar.
   - The Lab story must not overtake the MIZAN product story.

3. Arrownex is the operating and delivery organisation.
   - Arrownex sells and operates.
   - IIPI owns the method.
   - Assessment stays separate from remedy.

## MIZAN Positioning

MIZAN sits one plane above runtime control. Runtime governance watches the system; MIZAN watches the permission. It is not an observability dashboard, AI security scanner, compliance checklist, generic GRC platform, runtime agent-control platform, scoring engine or certification engine.

The product logic is:

- named claims
- named evidence
- named authorisation
- visible accountability
- expiry
- conditions
- reassessment when evidence or systems change

There must be no composite score, no trust gauge and no averaged red/amber/green maturity indicator.

## PPT Visual Characteristics

The supplied deck is the primary visual reference. It uses:

- deep navy full-slide backgrounds
- sparse institutional compositions with large negative space
- ivory text and cool blue-grey secondary text
- restrained warm gold linework and labels
- thin architectural strokes, rings and connected paths
- transparent planes and technical grid language
- a single architectural AI image used on the opening and closing slides
- high-contrast, presentation-grade typography using Dubai

The website should translate these qualities into web-native components, not reproduce slides.

## Extracted Palette

Observed repeatedly in PPTX shape XML:

- `#0A1D30` background dark
- `#12283F` background deep
- `#1B3A57` deep blue support
- `#C9A24B` restrained institutional gold
- `#E4C77E` soft gold
- `#F2EDE1` ivory text/surface
- `#9FB0C2` cool blue-grey
- `#000000` near-black

Dominant embedded image colours are near-black blue/navy, with warm light concentrated on the right side of the composition. The web hero should use the same asymmetry: text stability on the left, layered illuminated architecture on the right.

## Typography Recommendation

Use no external font loading. Prefer `Dubai` if installed, followed by `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, sans-serif. This follows the PPT rather than the Google-hosted Poppins/Inter pairing in the HTML source and keeps the site self-contained.

Arabic brand text should remain limited to the approved brand word `ميزان`, with `dir="rtl"` where useful. Do not attempt unapproved Arabic translation.

## Key Components

- Premium sticky navigation with transparent hero state and blurred navy scroll state.
- Full-screen hero with three translucent authority/accountability/machine planes and an illuminated gold path.
- Four failure-point problem section with restrained historical examples.
- Four-layer architecture visual: mandate and law, MIZAN, agent control, substrate.
- Authorisation record graph: public purpose, mandate, use case, authority envelope, named owner, evidence, capability, conditions, acceptance, expiry and change events.
- Permission lifecycle timeline: authorised, evidence ages, system changes, condition triggered, review required, re-authorise/condition/withdraw.
- Evidence provenance interaction with node metadata.
- MIZAN application mockup with no aggregate scores.
- Ecosystem orbit with MIZAN central, Qudra/Mu'tamad/Isnad operational, Misbar research-only.
- Current stage/collaboration section with precise caveats.
- Leadership section with separate roles and independence principle.
- Contact route for requesting a conversation.

## Content And Caveat Rules

- Do not state Abu Dhabi Polytechnic/IAT adopted or purchased MIZAN. State only that the supplied source describes a signed research collaboration with the Agentic AI for Public Purpose Lab.
- Do not state Core42 or TII adopted MIZAN, agreed to a pilot, or are partners. The source says proposed pilot only, not agreed.
- Do not state UCL backs, powers, endorses or partners commercially with MIZAN. Preferred wording: grounded in a doctoral research programme at UCL IIPP, using publicly available research and prospective academic collaboration.
- Do not invent customers, pilots, deployments, regulatory approvals, certifications, revenue, market share or endorsements.
- Abu Dhabi/UAE AI-government figures may be described as published government figures, not as MIZAN endorsements.
- Misbar is research-only, sandboxed, never sold, never priced, and its code is discarded by design.
- MIZAN is building its MVP, bootstrapped to date, with no external investment raised according to the supplied September 2026 deck.
