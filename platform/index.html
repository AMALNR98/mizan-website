Platform

One record for evidence, approval and review

MIZAN is being developed to help institutions connect an AI use to the evidence, people and conditions that support its approval. The record is designed to stay reviewable as the system and its operating environment change.

Core capabilities

What the platform helps you check and record

Define the approval.

Record the purpose, responsible owner, permitted actions, limits and review date.

Connect the evidence.

Bring relevant policies, assessments and technical reports into the approval record.

Record specialist review.

Keep specialist findings and required conditions visible to the decision owner.

Check organisational capability.

Consider institutional capability for this specific use.

Review changes.

Material changes trigger a review.

Recover the history.

Show what was known, what was reviewed, who decided, and the conditions attached.

Working with existing systems

Designed to use the evidence institutions already hold

MIZAN is designed to use evidence from existing risk, privacy, security and AI evaluation processes. Deployment requirements should be agreed privately for each pilot.

Positioning

Governance tools ask whether the system is safe. MIZAN asks whether the institution is entitled to let it act.
