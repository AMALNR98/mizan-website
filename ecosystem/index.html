Supporting instruments

Supporting the people and evidence behind the decision

MIZAN's wider research and development programme includes four supporting instruments. Their role is to strengthen an approval case for a specific AI use. Availability and pilot scope should be agreed with the team.

Four instruments

Secondary tools, not a competing front door

Qudra

Institutional capability. Examines whether the organisation has the mandate, people, processes and supervision needed for this AI use.

Explore capability

Mu'tamad

Human competence. Defines and assesses the knowledge and skills required of named reviewers and supervisors.

Explore accountable roles

Isnad

Evidence and knowledge provenance. Records where knowledge and evidence came from, who reviewed it, and when it needs updating.

Explore evidence sources

Misbar

Controlled research. Tests assumptions, boundaries and failure scenarios in a defined environment before wider use.

Explore research testing
