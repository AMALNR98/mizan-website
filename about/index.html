Connecting AI delivery with institutional responsibility

MIZAN is being developed through Arrownex's AI for Public Purpose Lab in the UAE. The Lab brings applied research and institutional assurance into practical AI delivery for government and regulated organisations.

Arrownex, the Lab and institutional responsibility

Arrownex provides the operating and delivery platform. The AI for Public Purpose Lab develops and tests the methods and product proposition. The institution using MIZAN keeps responsibility for its AI approval decisions.
