About MIZAN

Institutional governance for the age of AI agents.

MIZAN is designed around a simple enterprise requirement: every consequential AI use case should have a visible, evidence-based and accountable permission record.

Operating model

Arrownex operates and delivers the platform. IIPI maintains the institutional method. Assessment remains independent from remediation.

Purpose

Strengthen how institutions authorise, maintain and reassess AI-enabled operations.

Method

Connect mandate, evidence, accountability, capability, conditions and expiry in one governed record.

Delivery

Support enterprise integration, governance workflows and institutional operating change.

Independence

Keep assurance judgement separate from implementation and remediation services.

Leadership

Governance expertise meets enterprise technology delivery.
