How MIZAN works

MIZAN helps your team define the AI use, check the evidence, approve it, and review it when things change.

Six clear steps

From definition to review

Define

Set the AI use, decision boundary and responsible owner at a high level.

Evidence

Bring relevant existing evidence into view for the approval discussion.

Assess

Review whether the available evidence supports the proposed use.

Authorise

Record the human decision, conditions and review date.

Monitor

Keep the approval visible as the use and operating context change.

Reauthorise

Material changes trigger a review before the approval is continued or changed.

Change

If the AI use changes, review the approval

Material changes trigger a review before the AI use moves beyond its approved scope.

What can happen?

The approval can continue, be limited, be paused, or be stopped. MIZAN records the reason and review date in plain words.
