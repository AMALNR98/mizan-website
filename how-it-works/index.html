How it works

From evidence to accountable authorisation

The same six steps appear across the site: Define, Evidence, Assess, Authorise, Monitor and Reauthorise. The example below follows a student support assistant (constructed).

Six-step workflow

The human decision point stays visible

Step 1

Define

What you bring: Purpose, owner, permitted actions and limits.

Who is involved: Service owner and governance lead.

What MIZAN produces: A bounded use case and draft approval scope.

Step 2

Evidence

What you bring: Policies, privacy review, security review, accessibility testing and source list.

Who is involved: Evidence owners and specialist teams.

What MIZAN produces: A connected evidence set with source and review status.

Step 3

Assess

What you bring: The connected evidence set and identified gaps.

Who is involved: Relevant specialists and accountable reviewers.

What MIZAN produces: Findings, unresolved gaps and required conditions.

Step 4

Authorise

What you bring: Specialist findings and proposed conditions.

Who is involved: An authorised institutional decision owner.

What MIZAN produces: A worded decision, reasons, conditions and review date.

Step 5

Monitor

What you bring: Agreed signals such as source changes, incidents and complaints.

Who is involved: Operational owner with escalation routes.

What MIZAN produces: Signals attached to the relevant part of the approval.

Step 6

Reauthorise

What you bring: Affected evidence and change history.

Who is involved: Decision owner with relevant reviewers.

What MIZAN produces: Continue, restrict, suspend or withdraw the approval.

Running example

Student support assistant (constructed)

The assistant answers routine student questions using approved institutional information. Staff handle ambiguous questions and consequential decisions. The approval record defines the assistant's boundaries, evidence and review triggers.

Discuss a pilot

Illustrative concept using sample information. It does not represent a live deployment.

Student support assistant (constructed)

AI use
Student support assistant for routine questions.
Purpose
Answer questions using approved institutional information.
Accountable owner
Student services lead.
Allowed
Use approved sources and draft support responses.
Not allowed
Decide eligibility, discipline, progression or access to services.
Evidence
Source accuracy, student data protections, accessibility, staff readiness.
Decision
Authorised, with conditions
Conditions
Escalate ambiguous questions and consequential decisions to staff.
Review date
30 January 2027.
Material change

When the use changes, the permission comes back for review

If the assistant is proposed to start making eligibility decisions, the original guidance-only approval must be reassessed. The affected evidence, human decision boundary and accountable owner return to review before any new approval is recorded.

Possible outcomes

Continue, restrict, suspend or withdraw the approval. The outcome is recorded in words with the reason and review date, not as a score.
